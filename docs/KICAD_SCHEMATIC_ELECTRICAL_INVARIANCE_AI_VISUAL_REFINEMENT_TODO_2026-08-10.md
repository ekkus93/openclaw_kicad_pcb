## Governing rule

Do not enable model-directed schematic mutation until Phase A electrical invariance and Phase B transactional candidate safety are complete and green.

A visual refinement is successful only when the **actual post-edit KiCad artifact** is proven electrically equivalent to the authoritative Circuit IR and accepted pre-edit schematic baseline.

No silent fallback, approximate mutation, skipped hard verification, or `pass_with_warning` substitution is acceptable for production apply/refine mode.

# Final definition of done

This TODO is complete only when a poor-but-electrically-correct schematic can be safely analyzed by a vision-capable LLM, converted into explicit deterministic layout operations, applied to isolated candidates, verified against the actual KiCad artifact, and improved through a bounded iterative loop while preserving component inventory and exact electrical connectivity under the specification's invariance rules.

The implementation must demonstrate both sides of the contract:

- safe geometry-only changes are accepted when they genuinely improve the schematic; and
- deliberate component/connectivity/footprint/no-connect corruption is reliably rejected without damaging the known-good schematic.
